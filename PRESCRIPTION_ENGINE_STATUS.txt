📊 EXTRACTION CAPABILITIES

Per Medicine Extraction:

  • strength          → Dosage amount (650 mg, 500 mcg, etc.)
  • dosage_pattern    → Pattern string (1-0-1, BD, TDS, etc.)
  • frequency_per_day → Integer (1, 2, 3, 4)

  • prescription_date    → YYYY-MM-DD format
  • safety_flags         → 4 automated checks


🧠 DOSAGE INTELLIGENCE RULES (Implemented)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Pattern       → Frequency Per Day
  ───────────────────────────────
  1-0-1         → 2
  1-1-1         → 3
  OD            → 1 (Once Daily)
  BD            → 2 (Twice Daily)
  TDS           → 3 (Thrice Daily)
  QID           → 4 (Four times)


🔍 MULTI-STRATEGY EXTRACTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Strategy 1 (PRIMARY): Line-by-Line Comprehensive Scan
  • Scans every single line for medicine patterns
  • Looks ahead 2-3 lines for context
  • Highest confidence

Strategy 2 (SECONDARY): Structured Field Extraction
  • Extracts from "Composition:", "Medication:", etc.
  • Handles standardized formats
  • Medium confidence

Strategy 3 (FALLBACK): Aggressive Pattern-Based
  • Whole-text scan for any medicine names
  • Ensures NOTHING is missed
  • Lower confidence but complete coverage


🚨 SAFETY FLAGS (Automated)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  duplicate_medicines     → Same medicine listed twice
  unclear_dosage          → Missing frequency data
  missing_duration        → No treatment period
  handwritten_uncertain   → Low OCR quality / messy text
